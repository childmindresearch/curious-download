Writes a folder with export_info.json (study set-up, view settings, when the data was loaded, counts, the status legend), dashboard_cells.csv (one row per participant and column), dashboard_grid.csv (as shown), participants.csv (totals per participant) and occurrences.csv (every expected occurrence and completion). The export appears under Runs with a Show in Finder button.

How to read it: the schedule (default events, individual events, and their history) is expanded into the days and time windows each participant's phone offered an activity. Each completion is matched to the occurrence the phone recorded it for; older completions are matched by activity and time window. Curious keeps unfinished activities on the phone only, so "partly done" exists for flows alone. Click a cell for the details.
